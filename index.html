Expertise

My key areas of expertise.

My expertise spans the entire software development spectrum, from crafting intuitive web and mobile experiences to building robust desktop applications. Frontend Development

Frontend Development is the field that I have the most confidence and freedom on, I'm pretty much familiar with most of the technologies used in this fast paced field; here are some of the technologies I recently used: React.js & Next.js, Vue.js & Nuxt.js, Astro etc.

Backend Development

For Backend development I use multiple programming languages depending on the projects scale and needs, typically I use MERN stack for my projects, but when security matters I use Python, and when security, efficiency and development time matters my go-to option is PHP.
